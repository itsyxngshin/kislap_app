Account Deletion Request for Kislap

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your Kislap account and all associated data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can initiate the permanent deletion of your account using either of these two methods:

Method 1: Inside the App (Instant)

  1. Open the Kislap app on your device.
  2. Navigate to the Settings screen.
  3. Scroll down to the bottom and tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Confirm the permanent deletion prompt. Your data will be wiped instantly.

Method 2: Via Email (If you uninstalled the app)

If you no longer have the app installed, you can request data deletion via email. You must send the request from the exact email address associated with your Kislap account.

  • Email: snapsailinteractive@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Body: "I request the permanent deletion of my Kislap account and all associated data."

Email requests will be processed and confirmed within 7 business days.

Types of Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently purged from our active Supabase cloud servers and your local device cache:

  • Account Identity: Your full name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Financial Baselines: Your target monthly budget and utility tariff rates.
  • Household Inventory: All custom appliances, wattages, quantities, and usage schedules.
  • Billing History: All historical billing periods and usage records.

Data Retained & Retention Period

We do not retain any of your personal or household data after deletion.

  • Active Database: Data is purged immediately upon using the in-app deletion button, or within 7 days via email request.
  • Encrypted Server Backups: Any residual data inside encrypted automated server backups is completely overwritten and permanently destroyed within 30 days of the deletion request.
