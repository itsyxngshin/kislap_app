Compliance Standards: Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173), Google Play Data Safety, & Google API Services User Data Policy

1. Introduction & Data Privacy Principles

KISLAP ("we," "us," "our," or "the App") respects your fundamental right to privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, secure, and dispose of your personal and household energy data in strict compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012 of the Philippines), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), National Privacy Commission (NPC) circulars, and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

We process data strictly in accordance with the statutory principles of:

  • Transparency: You are fully informed of the nature, purpose, and extent of data processing prior to granting consent.
  • Legitimate Purpose: Data is processed exclusively for household electricity calculation, budget tracking, and load optimization.
  • Proportionality: We collect only the minimum dataset required to perform energy computations and synchronize your account.

2. Detailed Categories of Information We Collect

Data Category Specific Data Elements Collection Method & Scope
Identity & Authentication Data Full name, email address, user UUID, encrypted authentication tokens, and consent timestamps. Collected upon registration via Email/Password or Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0). Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed; plain-text passwords are never seen or stored by KISLAP.

What We Do NOT Collect: KISLAP does not collect GPS location data, contacts, microphone/camera recordings, payment card numbers, government IDs, or background device telemetry.

3. Purpose & Legal Basis of Processing

Under Section 12 of R.A. 10173, we process your data based on your explicit, freely given, and informed consent (captured via an affirmative checkbox during sign-up) for the following purposes:

  • Computing real-time daily and monthly kilowatt-hour (kWh) and Philippine Peso (₱) consumption projections.
  • Running the optimization algorithm to suggest reduced operating hours for non-essential appliances when projections exceed your monthly budget cap.
  • Maintaining historical billing period logs for month-over-month comparison.
  • Synchronizing your offline SQLite appliance records with your cloud account across multiple devices.
  • Generating downloadable Excel (.xlsx) optimization reports on your local device.

4. Data Storage Architecture & Cryptographic Security

  • Local Offline Storage (SQLite): If you use KISLAP in Guest Mode, or while offline, your data is stored inside a sandboxed SQLite database on your device's internal storage. Guest data is never transmitted to our servers unless you explicitly register and select "Merge to Cloud."
  • Cloud Database & Row Level Security (Supabase): For registered users, data is hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL). Every database table (profiles, appliances, recording_periods) is strictly governed by PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring that your cryptographic session token (auth.uid()) can only query, update, or delete your own records.
  • Encryption in Transit & At Rest: All API traffic between the App and our cloud servers is encrypted via Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2/1.3 / HTTPS). Cloud databases are encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards.
  • Automatic Session Cleanup: When you sign out of your account, KISLAP automatically wipes the local SQLite cache tables on that device to prevent unauthorized data exposure on shared phones.

5. Third-Party Sub-Processors & Zero-Monetization Guarantee

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal or household energy data with third-party data brokers or advertising networks. We share data solely with essential infrastructure sub-processors bound by strict data protection agreements:

  • Supabase Inc.: Cloud database hosting (PostgreSQL) and user authentication services.
  • Google LLC (Google Identity Services): OAuth 2.0 single sign-on authentication. KISLAP's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Vercel Inc.: Static web application hosting and content delivery.

6. Data Retention & Mandatory Account Deletion (Google Play & App Store Compliance)

We retain your personal and appliance data only for as long as your account remains active. You have the absolute right to permanently erase your account and all associated cloud records at any time:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: Navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account & Data and confirm the prompt. This triggers an automated backend routine (delete_user_account) that immediately and permanently purges your authentication record, profile, appliance inventory, and billing periods from our Supabase servers, and wipes your local SQLite device cache.
  • Web / Email Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the App and wish to delete your cloud data remotely, send an email from your registered email address to snapsailinteractive@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." Requests are processed and confirmed within 7 business days.

7. Your Statutory Rights Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act (R.A. 10173)

As a Data Subject under Sections 16–18 of the DPA, you are entitled to exercise the following rights at any time:

  1. Right to be Informed: To know whether and how your personal data is being processed.
  2. Right to Object: To withhold or withdraw consent to data processing at any time.
  3. Right to Access: To view your stored profile, tariff baselines, and appliance logs within the App.
  4. Right to Rectification: To correct, update, or modify inaccurate personal details, budgets, or appliance wattages directly in the App.
  5. Right to Erasure or Blocking: To suspend, withdraw, or order the permanent destruction of your personal data from our filing system.
  6. Right to Data Portability: To obtain an electronic copy of your appliance schedule and financial projections in a structured, commonly used format via the in-app Export to Excel (.xlsx) feature.
  7. Right to Damages & NPC Complaint: To be indemnified for damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, or unauthorized data use, and to lodge a formal complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) at https://privacy.gov.ph.

8. Children's Privacy

KISLAP is a household utility management tool not directed at children under the age of 13 (or minors under 18 without parental or guardian consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a minor has submitted personal data without verification of parental consent, we will promptly delete such information.
