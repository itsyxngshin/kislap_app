PRIVACY POLICY FOR KISLAP

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction and Scope

KISLAP ("we," "our," or "the App") is dedicated to protecting personal information in compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012 of the Philippines) and Google Play Data Safety Guidelines.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, and authentication tokens. Passwords are cryptographically hashed.
  • Financial Baseline: Target monthly electricity budget and utility tariff rates.
  • Appliance Load Data: Appliance names, operational wattage, quantities, and usage hours.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed based on your explicit consent solely for computing real-time kilowatt-hour estimates, generating power-saving schedules, and synchronizing your inventory across devices. We do not sell or trade your data to third parties.

4. Storage and Security

Data is encrypted in transit using standard TLS/HTTPS. Cloud data is stored securely in Supabase using Row Level Security (RLS) policies. Guest mode data remains strictly sandboxed within your local device's SQLite storage.

5. Account and Data Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time via the in-app "Delete Account" button in the Settings menu. You may also request deletion by emailing the address below.
